Good to know

Can I order online?

Yes. Order online for pickup or delivery, or call (972) 528-8570.

How late are you open?

Until 1 AM Monday to Thursday and until 2 AM on Fridays and Saturdays.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes: hummus, tabouli, fattoush, Arabic salad, lentil soup, falafel and fried halloumi.

Can you feed a group?

The Family Feast serves up to five, and the Combination Plate serves two.
